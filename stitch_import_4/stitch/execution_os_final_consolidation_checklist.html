OFFICIAL MASTER VISUAL SYSTEM: "CURATOR PRO" This is the single source of truth for all visual and structural decisions. * **Visual North Star:** Premium B2B productivity software. UNIFICATION & REJECTION RULES (STRICT) ### Standardize: * **Borders:** `1px border-slate-200/50` for light mode; `1px border-slate-800/50` for dark mode. * **Shadows:** Recessed `shadow-sm` or ambient `shadow-xl` for overlays only. * **Typography:** Manrope for headings, Inter for body/labels. Strict contrast ratios. ### REJECT: * **NO** left-side colored strips or accent bars on any component. * **NO** glowing neon rails or luminous borders. * **NO** page-level or inner-panel scrolling. * **NO** mobile-style stacked vertical layouts. --- ## 8.
